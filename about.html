The Genesis

Welcome to TOLEX RESTAURANT

TOLEX RESTAURANT was born out of a profound passion to honor African culinary heritage on a world-class luxury stage.

For generations, African cooking has been defined by rich communal traditions, bold indigenous spices, and hours of patient, loving preparation. At TOLEX, we preserve these sacred cooking traditions—infusing authentic firewood smokiness into our Jollof, hand-grinding pristine melon seeds for our Egusi, and blending coastal herbs for aromatic broths—while presenting each dish with refined, contemporary artistry.

Whether dining in our warm, amber-lit main hall or enjoying an intimate celebratory evening in our private VIP salon, every guest is enveloped in royal African hospitality that turns a meal into an indelible memory.

Custodians of Taste

Who We Are

We are a collective of dedicated master chefs, culinary historians, and hospitality professionals united by our love for African gastronomy.

Our kitchen brigade combines decades of authentic regional culinary knowledge with contemporary fine-dining disciplines. We refuse to cut corners: our stocks are slow-reduced from rich roasted bones, our locust beans (iru) are traditionally fermented, and our prime cuts of meat are tenderly marinated in indigenous yaji rubs for 24 hours prior to grilling.

Above all, we are proud ambassadors of African culture, welcoming local food connoisseurs and international travelers alike into our dining room as honored family members.

Purity & Excellence

Our Commitment to Quality

Quality is not an afterthought at TOLEX RESTAURANT—it is our absolute obsession.

We work directly with ethical local farmers and certified specialty purveyors across the African continent to source raw ingredients at the peak of their seasonal perfection. Our melon seeds are inspected seed by seed; our red palm oils are cold-pressed and pure; and our wild tiger prawns are sourced fresh from clean ocean waters.

Our culinary spaces maintain rigorous international food safety and hygiene protocols, ensuring that your dining pleasure is matched with peace of mind.
